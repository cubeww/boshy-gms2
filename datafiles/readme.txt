I Wanna Be The Boshy
====================

A precision platformer with tight jumps, traps, and boss fights.
Original game by Solgryn. GameMaker LTS 2026 port by Cube.

Default controls
----------------
Left / Right arrow  Move
Z                   Jump. Hold to jump higher. Press again in midair to double jump.
X                   Shoot. Shoot save blocks to save your progress.
R                   Restart from the last checkpoint or the room entrance.
S                   Skip supported cutscenes.
Esc                 Open Options during gameplay.
Enter               Confirm a menu selection.

You can change the gameplay keys under Options > Controls.

Recommended settings
--------------------
Turn on these settings in Options.

Auto Fire          Hold Shoot to fire repeatedly.
Low Input Latency  Apply horizontal input on the current frame for faster response.

steps to reproduce the issue, and a screenshot or video if available.
